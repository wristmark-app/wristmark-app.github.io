Accessibility

WristMark is designed to work with Apple's built-in accessibility technologies so more people can review meeting recordings and transcripts comfortably on iPhone.

Supported features

  • Works with VoiceOver and standard accessibility labels on navigation, recording, and review controls.
  • Uses system text, buttons, sheets, and lists where possible so it follows native iOS accessibility behavior.
  • Supports Dynamic Type and larger text sizes through iOS text scaling.
  • Supports Dark Interface on iPhone, with Apple Watch recording controls paired with the iPhone review flow.

AI content notes

Transcriptions and AI-generated summaries may be incomplete or inaccurate. Please review important meeting content manually, especially when using assistive technologies.

Need help?

If you encounter an accessibility barrier while using WristMark with VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Switch Control, or other assistive features, contact us at breet.jia@gmail.com.
